Turmite Zoo

A turmite is Langton's Ant generalized: a state string like RL means "on a white cell turn Right, on a black cell turn Left," then advance the cell's color and step forward. Same tiny rule, wildly different destinies — some rules churn out pure chaos forever, some settle into repeating highways or filled triangles, and the original RL spends ~10,000 steps looking like noise before suddenly discovering a diagonal "highway" and walking off in a straight line forever.
