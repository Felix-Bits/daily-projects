Elementary Cellular Automaton Explorer

Every row is computed from the one above it by a rule that only looks at three cells: left, self, right. There are exactly 256 such rules — this whole 8-bit number is the entire program. Click the truth-table tiles below to hand-edit it and watch the pattern change live.

Toroidal (wrap-around) edges, 181 columns. Try rule 30 (chaos — used by Mathematica as an RNG), rule 90 (a perfect Sierpinski triangle from XOR), rule 110 (proven Turing-complete), or rule 184 (traffic flow: cars move right unless blocked). Click any cell in the top row of the canvas to toggle it and re-run.
