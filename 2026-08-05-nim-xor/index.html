Nim

Three piles of stones. Take any number (at least one) from a single pile on your turn. Whoever takes the last stone wins. You go first — good luck.

The trick, laid bare

Write each pile's size in binary and XOR them together (a "nim-sum"). If the result is 000, whoever moves next is in a losing position against perfect play — every move breaks the balance, and the opponent can always restore it. Watch this update as you play.

No randomness in the AI's decisions once it's your move — only in how the piles start. If the nim-sum is already 0 when the game begins, the AI wins outright no matter what you do. If it starts nonzero, you hold a winning line — but you have to find it every single turn, and the AI never will let you back in once you slip.
