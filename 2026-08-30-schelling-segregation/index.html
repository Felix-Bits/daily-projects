Schelling's Segregation Model

Two groups of agents live on a grid. Each one only wants a modest fraction of its neighbors to share its color — drag the slider low enough and most agents are already happy with a totally mixed neighborhood. Yet even a mild preference, applied by everyone at once, tends to snowball into near-total separation. Nobody in the simulation is trying to segregate; it falls out of individually reasonable choices.
